\subsection{Three wordings, matched denominators}

Three checkpoints, three seeds each, three wordings of all eight prompts: the published scaffolded prompt, which lists the classical assumptions; a minimal prompt with the list removed; and a leading prompt that appends one hint paragraph stating the core idea, generated so that every judge field stays byte-identical across conditions.

The ordering holds on every checkpoint. A hint that states the answer is worth +0.28 on a benchmark whose published signal is 0.93. The authors' own comparison of scaffolded against minimal (0.93 against 1.38) used different sample counts and checkpoint sets and cannot be read as a sensitivity result.
