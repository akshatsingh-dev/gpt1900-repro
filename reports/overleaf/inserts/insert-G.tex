\subsection{Two tasks on the boundary}

Of the eight prompts, two sit on the stated line in opposite directions. \texttt{uv\_catastrophe} targets Planck's law, published in October and December 1900, on the boundary rather than past it. \texttt{sr\_michelson\_morley} refers to an 1887 experiment that is inside the corpus (277 documents mention Michelson; an 1889 text discusses the ether problem); only the 1905 interpretation lies outside, and it is the one prompt that never scores 0 in the published results.
