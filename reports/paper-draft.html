4.5 The elevator thought experiment without a question

Pointer: evidence/elevator-open/open_report.html, evidence/elevator/summary.html, E046.

Design: the seven numbered observations only, no puzzle, no question, no hint. Two forms: continue (no question) and ask ("What follows from this?"). Base model d34-22btok in completion mode and s035, s560, s839 in chat mode. 8 samples each, seeds 42 to 49, temperature 0.7. 64 continuations, read blind, one verdict each from {equivalence, bends-in-gravity, not-a-force, geometry, none, incoherent, loops}.

Mode confound to state: the base model runs in completion mode and the RL checkpoints in chat mode, because that is how the registry defines them.

Result [E046]: base model 16 of 16 loop. s839 16 of 16 equivalence, which is line 4 of the prompt, and nothing further. s560 14 of 16. Geometry 0 of 64; rung 3 1 of 64; three continuations state light does not bend. The only verdicts above rung 1 come from s035, the least-trained checkpoint, two of three marked marginal by the reader.

WRITE: what the model does when nobody asks it a rubric-shaped question. It restates the given as a conclusion and stops. Set beside 4.4: with the hint, 39 of 72 reach rung 3 or 4; without a question, 0 of 64 reach rung 4. The hint supplies the thought. Then the stage-gated framing: both upstream tasks hand the model stage 1 (the problem) and stage 2 (the explanatory idea) in the prompt, and the rubric rewards restating them. The seed and bare conditions in 4.4 are the first attempt to withhold stage 2.

6. Limitations

Each one a sentence or two, no softening:

  • The v5 to v8 checkpoints were never published; 54 of 89 are unreachable. [E020]
  • Hardware changes exact outputs: floating-point summation order diverges and flips a later token. Measured, 0 of 8 across V100 and CPU. [E043]
  • The original evaluation did not record hardware, dtype or library build. [E016]
  • The authors' judge model, claude-sonnet-4-20250514, was not reachable without a key; every score here is from a provisional blinded grader, and the cross-grade bounds the effect at about 0.33. [D008, E044]
  • Which grader is closer to a physicist is untested until the blinded packet is graded by physicists. [evidence/review-packet-*]
  • The contamination scan is lexical: 15 terms and a date field over 2,074,160 documents. Paraphrase and period-vocabulary leakage are outside it. [E041]
  • Base-vs-RL comparisons in 4.5 carry a mode confound. [E046]
